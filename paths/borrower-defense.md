**Frank's first choice** (D-004). Status: **not filed, as far as the files show.** Research and

## What it is

A request to cancel federal loans because **the school misled you or broke the law** in a way
that affected your decision to enroll or borrow. Compared to PSLF: PSLF is earned over ten years
of work, like a pension vesting. Borrower defense is a claim, more like a refund for a product
that was misrepresented. If it is approved, the loans from that school are discharged, and
payments already made on them can be refunded.

**Every one of Frank's loans is from the University of Phoenix** (NSLDS, Dec 2024), so a win would
cover the whole balance, including the 2013 consolidation, which was made entirely of Phoenix
loans.

## The class action he missed

**Sweet v. Cardona** (studentaid.gov printout, Dec 2024): applications pending by 22 June 2022
were the class; applications filed 23 June to 15 Nov 2022 were "post-class applicants." Phoenix
was on the list of schools. **Frank did not apply by 15 Nov 2022**, so he is in neither group. A
new application is an individual claim and has to make its own case. (Frank's notes, Jan 2025.)

  based partly on the FTC case. (Same printout.)
