Not a way out by itself. It matters because **the plan decides whether a month counts toward PSLF**
and how big the payment is.

## Why not to touch it casually

Frank's history with this loan includes a default, Fresh Start, a forbearance while SAVE was
blocked, and a balance that "reappeared." The setup works now. Any change here is a one-way door
until proven otherwise (`CLAUDE.md`): say what could be lost before anything moves.

## What could move the payment

- **Annual recertification** of income. Date (NEED).
- **Filing status.** Amanda's income is higher than Frank's. Filing jointly would likely raise the
  payment; filing separately may keep it on his income alone but costs tax credits. `financial`
  and a tax preparer own that comparison.
- **The 2025 law.** See `../reference/rules-as-of-2026.md`. As Claude remembers it, IBR stays
  available to current borrowers. Verify before relying on it.
